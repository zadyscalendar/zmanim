🕐 Zmanim — what to show, and which opinion
Every zman is calculated fresh for today's exact date and this exact location — nothing here is a stored table. Turn any one on or off, and pick the specific opinion your community follows. "Sunset / Candle Lighting" and "Nightfall / Havdalah" always show (they switch wording automatically for Friday/Motzei Shabbos).
🔔 Notifications
Get notified when a zman arrives, or when a reminder you added is due. Works best while the app is open or recently open — it may not fire if it's been fully closed a long time (especially on iPhone), since reliable delivery then needs a paid push server, which this app doesn't use.

Before we set up — one thing to know

You might notice this app's zmanim are off by a minute, or even just a few seconds, compared to other zmanim apps or calendars you've used before.

That's not a mistake — it's actually more accurate. This app calculates directly from your device's real GPS location, not from a general area or zip code the way many other zmanim apps and calendars do. Two spots in the same town can have very slightly different sunrise and sunset times — this app uses the exact one you're standing in, so small differences from other apps are a sign of extra precision, not an error.
