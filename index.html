Finding location…

This app always tries to use your real current location, quietly checking in the background whenever it's online and location is turned on — so the zmanim automatically update if you actually travel. If location is turned off or unavailable, it simply keeps using the last real location it had, with nothing to configure. The Home button always returns to wherever this device first really connected from — there's nothing to type in; it names itself.

Every zman below is calculated fresh for the exact date and exact location, every time — none of this is a stored table. Turn any of them on or off, and pick the specific opinion your community follows for it. "Sunset / Candle Lighting" and "Nightfall / Havdalah" are always shown (they automatically switch wording on Friday/Motzei Shabbos as before) — everything else is optional.

Choose which zmanim should notify you. This works while the app is open or recently open in the background — it may not fire if the app has been fully closed a long time (especially on iPhone), since that would need a paid push-notification server, which this app doesn't use.

These times use the common GRA-based calculation for your current location's coordinates. Please confirm exact candle-lighting and havdalah practice with your Rav — this device is for convenient reference, not for psak halacha.
